**AWS Pricing Calculator** is ==a free web-based planning tool available at the AWS Pricing Calculator Portal== that enables you to create and model cost estimates for running workloads and services across the AWS infrastructure. [[1](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pricing-calculator/latest/userguide/what-is-pricing-calculator.html), [2](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/pricing-calculator.html)]

---

Core Pricing & Estimate Tiers

|Feature / Estimate Type|Pricing|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**Workload Estimates**|**Free**|Interactively model costs for specific applications, resources, or new architectures.|
|**Bill Estimates**|**5 Free / Month** ($2.00 per additional estimate)|Estimate the cost impact of usage changes or commitments on an entire organizational bill.|
|**Exporting Data**|**Free**|Download generated configurations and resource-level details in CSV, JSON, or PDF formats.|

---

How AWS Pricing Calculator Operates

- **Public vs. In-Console Experience:** The public web version operates without requiring an AWS account. The authenticated version inside the [AWS Billing and Cost Management Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/) lets you import actual historical usage data and manage advanced pricing discounts.
    
    [[1](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/pricing-calculator.html), [2](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws-cloud-financial-management/create-your-personalized-cost-estimate-with-aws-pricing-calculator/)]
- **Rate Options:** Allows you to view baseline On-Demand rates, rates reflecting volume discounts, or configurations incorporating active purchase commitments like [AWS Savings Plans](https://aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/) and Reserved Instances. [[1](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2025/05/aws-pricing-calculator-discounts-purchase-commitments/), [2](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/pricing-calculator.html)]
- **Transparency & Calculations:** Provides a "Show calculations" breakdown for individual services so you can inspect how metrics, operational hours, and storage tiers factor into your estimated monthly or 12-month spend. [[1](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pricing-calculator/latest/userguide/getting-started.html), [2](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pricing-calculator/latest/userguide/what-is-pricing-calculator.html)]
