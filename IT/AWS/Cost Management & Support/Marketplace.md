**AWS Marketplace** is ==a digital catalog and online store managed by [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/)== that allows customers to find, buy, deploy, and manage third-party software, data products, and professional services that run on Amazon Web Services. [[1](https://aws.amazon.com/video/watch/a46f7f32260/), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=art8eIAd98A&t=345)]

---

Core Delivery Methods & Pricing Structure

|Delivery Method|Pricing Options|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**SaaS Subscriptions & Contracts**|Free trial, hourly, monthly, annual, multi-year|Software hosted by the vendor; uses entitlement or metering APIs.|
|**Amazon Machine Images (AMIs)**|Hourly or annual instance rates|Pre-configured virtual machine images deployed on [Amazon EC2](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/).|
|**Container Products**|Pay-as-you-go or fixed term|Container images for [Amazon ECS](https://aws.amazon.com/ecs/) or [Amazon EKS](https://aws.amazon.com/eks/).|
|**Professional Services**|Custom milestone or fixed fee|Consulting, implementation, and training services sold by partners.|

How AWS Marketplace Operates

- **Consolidated Billing:** All software charges and subscription fees land directly on your standard monthly AWS invoice, simplifying corporate procurement and vendor onboarding. [[1](https://aws.amazon.com/video/watch/a46f7f32260/), [2](https://www.reddit.com/r/aws/comments/1bvyt8a/do_people_actually_use_aws_marketplace/)]
- **Private Offers:** Allows vendors and buyers to negotiate custom pricing, flexible payment schedules, and tailored contract terms outside of public list prices. [[1](https://clazar.io/guides/aws-marketplace), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HU_SJTFHcGU)]
- **EDP and Commitments:** Enterprise Discount Plan (EDP) commitments or annual spend agreements can often be drawn down by purchasing eligible software through the Marketplace. [[1](https://www.reddit.com/r/aws/comments/1bvyt8a/do_people_actually_use_aws_marketplace/)]
- **Automated Deployment:** Offers 1-click launches, CloudFormation templates, and API integrations that let applications provision and configure instantly within your [Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (VPC)](https://aws.amazon.com/vpc/). [[1](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/pricing-overview.html)]
