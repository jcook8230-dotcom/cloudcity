Amazon S3 solves the problem of **storing and managing unstructured data at infinite scale without running out of disk space**.

- **Buckets:** Solve the problem of a flat, global namespace where files (objects) can be stored and retrieved over standard HTTP URLs from anywhere in the world.
- **Storage Classes:** Solve the problem of **overpaying for inactive data**. By offering tiers like Standard, Intelligent-Tiering, and Infrequent Access, S3 lets you pay less for data you do not look at every day.
- **Lifecycle Policies:** Solve the problem of **manual data cleanup and migration**. They automate the process of shifting aging files into cheaper storage classes or deleting them after a set number of days.
